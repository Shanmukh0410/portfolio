Faculty-advised research · Sep 2025 to present

Stratosyn (in progress)

Collect once, store centrally, correlate everything, then act. Plenty of tools inventory AWS resources. Correlating inventory, security posture, compliance and cost into one operational model, and safely remediating from it, is the hard part, and it is what the research is about.

The discovery engine enumerates accounts through AWS Organizations, assumes a read-only role per account, and collects every field boto3 returns rather than a fixed column set. The schema for a service becomes the union of every key seen anywhere in the organisation, null-filled where a resource omitted it, because a DynamoDB table with deletion protection off has no such key, and a Lambda with no VPC omits the block entirely. Raw NDJSON lands partitioned in S3; Glue and Athena give schema-on-read.
